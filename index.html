Questions

Before you reach out.

How does a pilot work?

You tell us your channels, volume, and hours. We handle a small, defined slice of your real work, and we agree in advance how long it runs and what success looks like. You review the results before deciding whether to scale.

How quickly can you start?

After a short scoping call, we give you a realistic start date in the written quote. It depends on the service, the team size, and how much training on your product is needed.

Which tools do you work in?

We work inside your own helpdesk, chat, or cloud tools. Tell us your stack in the enquiry, and we'll confirm what we support before you commit.

How do you protect our data?

We sign a confidentiality agreement before we see anything, limit access to the tools the work needs, and remove access when the engagement ends.

What are the contract terms?

Terms depend on the engagement model: a pilot, a dedicated team, or a fixed-scope project. We agree them in writing before work starts.

What if a team member isn't the right fit?

Tell us. We'll address it with the team, including changing who is assigned to your account.

How do I get a quote?

Send an enquiry below with your service, rough volume, and timeline. We'll follow up, then send a written quote after a short call.
